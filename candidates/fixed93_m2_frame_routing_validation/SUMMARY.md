# Single M2 historical equivalence only

The independent actual review confirmed one completed, unsampled M=2 synthetic
N1 Attention run under the source-attested owner-frame routing contract. This
excerpt reports that historical result; it does not rerun or independently
reconstruct it and grants no authority for another invocation.

## Recorded result

- The review verified 51 closed artifacts totaling 6,011,752 bytes and both
  sealed event streams. Those artifacts are not distributed in this package.
- All 33 output/field/presence file hashes matched the pinned historical review;
  transport also matched. These are 33 files, not 33 independent field domains.
- Attention had 8,565 records and Wholeblock had 2,211 records. Their recorded
  semantic differences were empty after the documented opaque-identity handling.
  This is not raw stream byte equality: process-local identity differences remain.
- The review checked 11,011 opaque-ID difference rows, four long-lived forward
  and reverse identity mappings, and 1,515 generic long-lived references.
  Short-lived alias equivalence was not established. Tags and epochs were not
  normalized. Timing, variant and instrumentation/observer identity report keys
  were excluded from semantic comparison.
- Main-loop CPU was 8.033911631 seconds and wall time was 8.280947779 seconds.
  Outer wall time was 17.369538329 seconds, with return code 0. The main loop
  includes the real owner, two observers, codec and transport checks; it excludes
  construction and seal I/O. A single historical comparison is not a repeated,
  contemporaneous benchmark or a full-size runtime forecast.

## Acceptance boundary

Attention observation completed, but Wholeblock remains
`INCOMPLETE_WHOLEBLOCK`, with no fullblock acceptance. Public advance, public
jobs/completions/PC and SiLU work were not exercised. Unobserved domains include
expanded4096 Matrix-bank-per-issue and SiLU nested17 exp/pre-BF16 multiply work.
The observer did not independently verify matrix-binary numerical provenance.

This is single-M2 historical observer equivalence only. It is not an independent
numerical oracle, official input/reference acceptance, M128 qualification,
complete declared schedule/cache acceptance or RTL PASS. It provides no
permission to increase scope, loosen limits or launch another run.

The routing policy deliberately omits eligible off-hook thread, owner, locals,
ancestry and frame-code audit sampling. Original control/evidence boundaries
remain, but transient drift restored before a retained boundary may escape or
be detected later. Matching event results do not establish equivalence to the
predecessor's per-line integrity sampling.
